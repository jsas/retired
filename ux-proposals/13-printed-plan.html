A Plan for Your Retirement

Prepared the 30th of August, 2026
The Arrangement

You are fifty-five today, and you have $850,000 set aside.1 You mean to stop working at 62,2 and until then you will add $25,000 a year.3 At 65 the government begins to add $22,000 a year4 — a small tributary that never runs dry, and asks nothing of you.

The Household

From 62 the household lives on $85,000 a year,5 drawn down from what you have saved. Nothing else is assumed: no inheritance, no property sold, no part-time work, no windfall. The figure is deliberately plain — it is what you spend, not what you hope to spend.

The Weather

Markets will not be kind in every year of a thirty-year retirement, so we have run the same plan three ways.6 In ordinary weather your money lasts until you are 89. In a storm, until you are 80. In a kind stretch, 110+.7 The storm is the honest one to plan around, and it is the one we would put our name to.

In Closing

Conclusion. As written, this plan does not hold. Your money lasts until you are 89 — six years short of the 95 you have in mind — and nothing remains at the end. Working two more years would close the gap. We would not sign this plan as it stands; we would sign the one with that change in it.
