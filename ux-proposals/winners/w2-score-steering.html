Where the number comes from

Time 40% of the score

Full marks if the money lasts past your planned age; partial credit for how far short it falls.

Buffer 35%

What's left at the end. Leftovers are room for care bills, a bad year, or help for the kids.

Weather 25%

The same plan re-run in stormy markets. A plan that only works with luck scores lower.

It never rewards luck, and it never hides a failing plan: if the money runs short, the page says so, with the one change that would close the gap.
