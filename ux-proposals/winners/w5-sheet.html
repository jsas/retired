Why a spreadsheet?

01

No black box

Click any year and the formula bar says its math in words: "started with $1.2M, growth added $35k, you spent $85k." If you can't check it, you shouldn't trust it.

02

Honest answers

If the money runs out, the row turns red and the verdict says so — then tells you the one change that closes the gap. No hedging, no forty-page PDF.

03

Take it with you

A lot of people like spreadsheets. Export the whole projection — every year, every account — to .xlsx or .csv and keep playing in Excel. It's your plan.

Your data never leaves this device.

No servers, no accounts. Scenarios, settings and results live only in this browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked. Export a backup or a spreadsheet any time; it works offline too.
