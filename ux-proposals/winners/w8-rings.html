Close your rings → Opens the planner — free, no account, 2 minutes.
01

Outlast

The big one. If you're planning to 95, the money should get there too. This ring closes only when it does — and tells you exactly how far short it falls when it doesn't.

02

Cushion

Reaching 95 with nothing left is cutting it fine. This ring fills as your leftover grows — three years of spending in reserve closes it.

03

Weatherproof

The same plan, replayed in stormy markets. If the plan only works when the weather is kind, this ring refuses to close.

Your data never leaves this device.

No servers, no accounts, nothing uploaded. Your plan lives only in this browser's local storage — export a backup file if you switch devices.
