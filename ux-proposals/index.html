Every one is a live, interactive mockup of the same idea — a retirement plan reduced to one honest answer — expressed forty different ways. Drag things, click numbers, change the weather.

Reading the room: 01–03 are the proven middle ground; 04–06 the metaphors; 07–20 the wave-1 sleek register; 21–40 the wave-2 expansion (paper & ink, data-forward, journey, atmosphere, structured). dashboard.html merges the winners into a full page; REQUIREMENTS.md is the contract for building any of them.

Sample plan runs slightly short on purpose (money out at 89) — every design must say so honestly and offer the one change that fixes it. Favorites can merge: e.g. 01's hero + 12's score + 08's timeline.
